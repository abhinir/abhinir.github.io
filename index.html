TRAJECTORY OPTIMIZATION · FEEDBACK CONTROL

Box-iLQR

Fast constrained trajectory optimization with feedback

Box-iLQR is a constrained nonlinear trajectory-optimization framework that combines iterative Linear Quadratic Regulator (iLQR) with barrier methods to efficiently compute locally optimal trajectories and time-varying feedback policies.

The framework is designed for constrained nonlinear systems where computational efficiency, feasibility, and robust closed-loop execution are important.

REINFORCEMENT LEARNING · SAFE CONTROL

Safe Reinforcement Learning

Learning control policies with explicit safety constraints

We investigate reinforcement-learning controllers for safety-critical nonlinear systems by combining PPO and TD3 with Control Barrier Function based safety filtering and benchmarking them against SAC.

The policies are trained on a unicycle obstacle-avoidance environment and evaluated on unseen obstacle configurations to study safety, learning performance, and generalization.
