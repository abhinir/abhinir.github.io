TRAJECTORY OPTIMIZATION · FEEDBACK CONTROL

Box-iLQR

Fast constrained trajectory optimization with feedback

Box-iLQR is a constrained nonlinear trajectory-optimization framework that combines iterative Linear Quadratic Regulator (iLQR) with barrier methods to efficiently compute locally optimal trajectories and time-varying feedback policies.

The framework is designed for constrained nonlinear systems where computational efficiency, feasibility, and robust closed-loop execution are important.
